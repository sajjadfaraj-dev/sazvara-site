System Diagnostic

Describe the system, not the solution.

The production intake endpoint will be connected during the domain launch. For the foundation release, use the checklist below to prepare a high-signal project brief.

What exists now?

Website, product, workflow, prototype, plugin, spreadsheet, automation or unfinished implementation.

What is going wrong?

The failure, bottleneck, manual work, blocked release or business constraint that is creating urgency.

What should be true instead?

The observable outcome that would make the work successful.

What cannot be broken?

Production data, existing behavior, deadlines, permissions, legal constraints, integrations or operational dependencies.

Foundation status: project intake is intentionally not collecting data until the production domain and privacy flow are connected.
