Why the studio exists

Role in the work

The founder role is not limited to implementation. It includes deciding what problem is actually being solved, turning that into requirements and acceptance criteria, coordinating fast execution, reviewing UX and technical trade-offs, checking evidence and owning the release decision.

AI is used extensively for research, comparison and implementation, but claims about quality or completion are expected to come from observable evidence: source history, validation output, public artifacts or measured production behavior.
